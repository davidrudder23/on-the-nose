On The Nose

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

On The Nose is a guitar tuner app for Android, published by Dave Rudder. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: On The Nose does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Microphone

The app uses your device's microphone to hear the note you are playing. The sound is analysed on your device, as it arrives, to work out its pitch. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app only listens while it is open on screen, and stops when you leave it.

No internet access

The app does not request permission to use the internet, so it has no way to send information off your device.

What is stored on your device

The only thing the app saves is the tuning you last selected (for example, Standard or Drop D), so it can be restored the next time you open the app. This setting stays on your device. If you have Android's device backup turned on, it may be included in that backup along with your other app settings. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

No ads, analytics or accounts

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party services that collect data. It does not ask you to create an account or provide any personal details.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/davidrudder23/on-the-nose/issues.
